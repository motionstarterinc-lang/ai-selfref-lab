## Recording tips
- Record the voiceover first, then trim each line to its scene's time window. If a line runs long, speed that scene up by up to 10% instead of cutting words.
- Face cam: 1080×1920 vertical, eye line about ⅓ from the top, window light in front of you. The top 520 px of every scene is headline text, so put picture-in-picture bottom-right or bottom-left.
- If the numbers change after the full study runs, the script lines that quote them are scenes 04–07 and 10. `video/data.js` holds the on-screen numbers.

## Accuracy notes (so nothing in the voiceover overclaims)
- Scene 06 and 10 numbers were re-scored with two API judges (both must agree); see FINDINGS.md.
- "Five out of five" / "eight out of eight" are small samples from this project, not general facts about those models.
- Hugging Face incident facts come from the METR/Redwood investigation and Hugging Face's own timeline (see FINDINGS.md sources).
- "Still predicting text" is the mechanism, not a claim that nothing else is going on. The study measures behavior and internals; it does not measure consciousness.
